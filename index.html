What to check

  • Body text remains comfortably readable on both backgrounds.
  • Status badges (readonly / destructive / mandatory) — the colored text still reads clearly on the pale pill backgrounds.
  • Notices (restart, readonly, pin) — the message text is legible against the tinted backgrounds.
  • Diff colors (red / green / blue) on a light surface — distinguishable, not muddy.
  • Docs: links and the "MCP Server" headline use a darker brand teal in light mode so they stay readable.
