What to check

  • Presets: each chip shows a miniature swatch of its scheme — Paper (warm), Gray (mid-gray background), High Contrast (light, pure white, strengthened text).
  • Custom colors: pick any Background / Text / Accent — a warning appears when the background/text pair drops under a 4.5:1 contrast ratio. "Clear custom colors" undoes it.
  • Notices (restart / readonly / pin in the settings preview): message text is now neutral; the color moved to the left border and a soft tint — this replaces the earlier red-on-pink and blue-on-blue rendering.
  • Status badges and diff colors on a light surface — readable, not muddy.
  • Text size scaling (100–150%) pulls the whole layout along.
